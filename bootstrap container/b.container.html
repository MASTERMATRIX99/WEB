My favorite hobby is gardening, an activity that has become an essential part of my daily routine. Initially, I started gardening with my mother on weekends, planting simple flowers and herbs. Gradually, my interest deepened as I learned more about different types of plants, soil types, and gardening techniques. Now, I maintain a small garden with seasonal flowers, medicinal herbs, and vegetables. This hobby brings me closer to nature and helps me reduce daily stress and anxiety. Spending time with plants in the morning refreshes my mind, improves concentration, and positively impacts my studies. Besides, I enjoy watching the plants grow, bloom, and bear fruits, which gives a sense of achievement. Gardening also promotes a sense of responsibility as it requires care, discipline, and consistent effort. It has environmental benefits as well because plants help increase oxygen levels and reduce pollution around us. Sharing organic vegetables and flowers with family and friends fills me with pride. My hobby has also improved my knowledge of botany and environmental science, which aids my school projects. Overall, gardening as a hobby enriches my life physically and mentally and nurtures empathy for living beings. I believe every student should have a hobby as it promotes creativity, reduces mental pressure, and helps develop essential skills beyond academics, making life more productive and enjoyable.
